# Asset Licensing

The repository’s software license applies to original source code unless a file states otherwise.

Game screenshots, third-party logos, trademarks, fonts, audio, video, reference images, and other externally sourced media are **not** relicensed by the repository’s software license. They remain subject to their respective owners’ rights and may require replacement or separate permission before redistribution.

Original project artwork may be distributed only when its file, manifest, or accompanying notice explicitly says so.
